# Partner Bank vs. Bank für Vermögen (BfV) — Strategic Positioning

> Owner's analysis of where Partner Bank AG stands against Bank für Vermögen (BfV)
> and how to position and grow. This is the strategic brief; the competitor website
> teardown (see `data/REPORT.md` once the scrape has run) provides the supporting
> evidence.

## Core conclusion

Partner Bank **already has many of the capabilities** of Bank für Vermögen (BfV) —
but it **does not communicate them nearly as well**. The opportunity is positioning
and communication, not building new capability from scratch.

## Recommendations

1. **Don't copy BfV.** Instead, position Partner Bank as *the Austrian private
   banking platform for independent wealth advisors and long-term investors*.

2. **Make advisors the primary customer.** Make it obvious that the bank exists to
   help advisors grow their businesses.

3. **Explain benefits, not technology.** Instead of talking about *Finvest* or
   *Hybriportal*, show how they help advisors save time, stay compliant, and serve
   clients better.

4. **Create a structured advisor journey:**
   inquiry → qualification → academy → onboarding → first clients → ongoing support.

5. **Use physical gold as a major differentiator.** Position it as a tool for wealth
   preservation and client conversations — not simply another product.

6. **Make the Partner Bank Academy public-facing.** Attract new advisors through
   courses, webinars, certifications, and education.

7. **Build a strong digital marketing strategy:** dedicated advisor landing pages,
   SEO content, LinkedIn campaigns, and educational comparison pages.

8. **Help advisors market themselves:** provide compliant content, conversation
   guides, graphics, and marketing support — not just banking services.

9. **Implement a 90-day action plan** that includes:
   - Redesigning the advisor section of the website
   - Creating lead forms
   - Visually explaining the platform
   - Launching advisor campaigns

## The strategic mindset shift

Shift the company's strategic mindset:

> Instead of asking **"How do we get more clients?"**, ask
> **"How do we make our advisors more successful so they naturally bring in more
> clients?"**

This is presented as the **biggest growth opportunity**.

## How this connects to the BfV website teardown

The competitor scrape (`scrape_bfv.py` → `data/`) is meant to give concrete,
presentable evidence for the recommendations above, especially:

- **How many landing pages BfV runs** and what each one targets (recommendation 7).
- **Where BfV captures leads** (forms) and how their advisor journey is structured
  (recommendations 4 & 9).
- **How BfV explains its platform** — benefits vs. technology language
  (recommendation 3).
- **Whether their academy/education content is public-facing** (recommendation 6).

Use those findings to show, side by side, where Partner Bank can communicate its
existing strengths better rather than build new ones.
